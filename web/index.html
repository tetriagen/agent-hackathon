Know what to ask.

Between a provider’s bill and an insurance explanation, it can be hard to see how the numbers fit together. ClearBill helps you review the details and prepare a written request for answers.

01 / Compare

Check totals that don’t add up.

Compare reported payments with their breakdown. See the difference and the source amounts to verify in your PDF.

02 / Review

Look for repeated services.

Find extracted rows with matching dates, service codes, quantities and charges. Check whether they represent separate services or a possible duplicate.

03 / Ask

Leave with a letter ready to edit.

Turn findings into a request for clarification. Add your details, download the draft and send it when you’re ready.
